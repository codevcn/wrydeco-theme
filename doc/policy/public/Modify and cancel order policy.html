01

The 6-Hour Modification & Cancellation Window

You have a 6-hour courtesy window from the exact moment your order is placed to request any changes or a complete order cancellation. During this initial timeframe, your order details are verified by our team and timber preparation has not yet been locked in.

All cancellation requests received strictly within this 6-hour window will be honored unconditionally and refunded in full to your original payment method with zero cancellation fees.

02

Permitted Order Modifications

Within the 6-hour window, you may request changes to your order parameters without penalty, including:

Delivery Address Correction Recipient Contact Details Wood Finish or Tone Selection Product Size Adjustment Quantity Modification

Note on price adjustments: If modifying your order results in a higher or lower product value, our customer service team will issue a partial refund or send an updated invoice link for the difference prior to production.

03

Custom Commissions & Made-to-Order Pieces

Many WRYDECO sculptures and bookcases are tailored to bespoke customer dimensions or private design consultations. For custom-commissioned pieces, the 6-hour courtesy window serves as the final opportunity to alter design schematics or cancel free of charge.

Once production blueprints and bespoke timber cutting have begun following design confirmation, custom and made-to-order commissions cannot be cancelled, modified, returned, or refunded (Final Sale), as detailed in our Return & Refund Policy.

In rare emergency situations where a customer insists on halting a bespoke order prior to packaging, WRYDECO retains a mandatory 30%–50% non-refundable custom production fee to offset bespoke architectural drafting, irreversibly dimensioned hardwood lumber, and merchant processing fees. Finished or crated bespoke pieces remain strictly 100% non-refundable.

Artisanal preparation notice

Our organic hardwood materials are individually selected for unique grain paths, natural live edges, and structural integrity. Because raw lumber is allocated promptly to uphold our 15–20 business day processing timeframe, our team enforces the 6-hour window strictly to prevent material waste.

04

Requests After 6 Hours & Post-Capture Policy

Captured Orders in Production: Once our concierge team confirms your order details and captures payment, the piece officially enters active handcrafting and timber milling. At this stage, orders are considered final and non-cancellable.

Discretionary Exceptions (Prior to Dispatch): In the rare event that an exceptional cancellation request for a standard catalog piece is accepted at WRYDECO’s sole discretion after payment capture, the refund will be subject to a 15% administrative and material restocking fee. This fee covers non-refundable payment processor transaction fees, administrative handling, and raw lumber allocation costs.

Orders Dispatched / Shipped: Once an order has been crated and transferred to the freight carrier (e.g., DHL or specialized freight partners), cancellation is strictly impossible. Customers must receive delivery and refer to our Return & Refund Policy.

05

Refund Processing & Fee Structure

How refunds and cancellations are handled depends directly on order and capture status:

• Pre-Capture Cancellations (Within Window): If your cancellation is received while payment is in the temporary authorization stage, the authorization is immediately voided. You receive a 100% full release of funds at $0 fee.

• Approved Post-Capture Exceptions: If an exceptional cancellation is approved after funds have been captured, the net refund (minus the 15% restocking/processing fee) will be issued to your original payment method within 1–2 business days. Please allow up to 3 additional business days for your banking institution to post the credit.

• Merchant-Initiated Cancellations: If WRYDECO is unable to fulfill your order due to unforeseen material shortages or delivery restrictions, you will receive an immediate 100% full refund with no deductions.

06

How to Request an Order Modification or Cancellation

To ensure your request is reviewed within the 6-hour window, please contact our concierge support team promptly following these steps:

1

Send an email directly to support@wrydeco.com with the subject line: "URGENT: Order Cancellation / Modification Request - #[Your Order Number]".

2

State clearly in the email body your full name, order number, and the exact changes requested (e.g., cancellation or updated shipping address/specifications).

3

Alternatively, initiate an instant session via our on-site Live Chat during business hours for priority handling.

Applicable Consumer Rights: This policy does not limit or exclude any non-waivable statutory consumer rights provided under the laws of your state, country, or jurisdiction. Where local legal statutes require different mandatory cancellation or cooling-off rights, such legal rights will prevail.
