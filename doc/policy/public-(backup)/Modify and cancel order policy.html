WRYDECO Policy

Modify & Cancel Order

At WRYDECO, each piece is handcrafted from natural solid wood with dedicated care. We understand that plans can change, and we offer a courtesy window for you to request order modifications or cancellations before preparation begins.

6-Hour Request Window Complimentary Modifications Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Helpful Notice

Please contact us promptly within 6 hours.

To help ensure your requests can be accommodated before our workshop coordinates lumber selection and hand-crafting, please submit any changes or cancellation requests within 6 hours of order placement.

01

Modification or Cancellation Window

You have a 6-hour window from the time of placing your order to request any modifications or cancellations. After this period, the order may already be in progress with our workshop and cannot be changed.

02

Requesting Changes

To modify or cancel your order within the allowed timeframe, contact our customer support team promptly. Email us at support@wrydeco.com with your order number and the specific details of the changes you need (such as shipping address update, contact details, or finish/size options).

Please ensure your request is submitted within the 6-hour window for timely processing. You can also reach out to our team via on-site Live Chat during business hours.

03

Handling Shipped Orders

If your order has already been shipped, modifications or in-transit cancellations are not possible. In such cases, you will need to wait for delivery and then follow our Return & Refund Policy for any adjustments or returns. This policy ensures you can still receive support even if the order has been dispatched.

04

Refunds for Canceled Orders

For eligible orders canceled within the specified 6-hour timeframe, WRYDECO will promptly issue a 100% refund to your original payment method. Once the refund is issued, most banks and card issuers typically take 5–10 business days to process and post the credit to your account. Processing times may vary depending on your financial institution or payment provider.

Contact Us

Questions regarding your order?

If you have any questions or need assistance with an existing order, please contact our customer support team. We are dedicated to providing you with the best possible support.
