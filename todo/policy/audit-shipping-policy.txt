WRYDECO Shipping Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

At WRYDECO, each piece is carefully prepared before shipment. Many of our products are handcrafted, sculptural, made to order, oversized, or require specialized handling, so preparation and fulfillment may take longer than standard retail shipping.

Shipping at a Glance
Standard Shipping Cost: $0.00 for eligible orders
Minimum Order Requirement: None
Processing Time: Approximately 15–20 business days
Transit Time: Approximately 3–5 business days after shipment
Estimated Total Time: Approximately 18–25 business days for standard orders where all required order information is complete
Shipping Coverage: Eligible domestic and international destinations available at checkout

Processing and delivery timeframes are estimates and are not guaranteed arrival dates.

1. Shipping Destinations

WRYDECO accepts orders for delivery to eligible domestic and international destinations worldwide.

Shipping availability may depend on:

Destination country or region
Accessibility of the delivery address
Product dimensions and weight
Carrier coverage
Local delivery restrictions
Specialized handling requirements
Other logistical conditions

International orders are accepted through checkout where delivery is available.

If additional information is required to confirm delivery to your address, WRYDECO may contact you after your order is placed.

2. Free Standard Shipping

WRYDECO provides complimentary standard shipping ($0.00) on all orders accepted for delivery to eligible destinations.

There is no minimum order value required for free standard shipping.

Complimentary shipping applies to the standard delivery method selected by WRYDECO and our logistics partners.

Optional services requested by the customer, including expedited delivery, specialized delivery, installation, storage, redelivery, or other additional services, may be subject to separate charges where available.

Any optional additional charge will be communicated before the related service is confirmed.

3. Order Processing Time

Please allow approximately 15–20 business days for standard order processing before shipment.

Processing may include:

Order preparation
Production coordination
Quality inspection
Product finishing
Packaging
Fulfillment coordination

Business days generally mean Monday through Friday, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and applicable public holidays.

For standard orders where all required information is complete at the time of purchase, the estimated processing period generally begins after the order is successfully placed and payment is authorized.

If required shipping information, customization details, specifications, measurements, customer approvals, or other necessary information are incomplete or delayed, processing may be paused or extended until the required information is received.

Custom, personalized, made-to-order, oversized, or special-order products may require additional production time.

Where a different product-specific lead time is displayed on the product page, quotation, order confirmation, or written agreement, that specific lead time will apply.

4. Estimated Transit Time

After an order has been processed and transferred to the selected carrier or logistics provider, estimated transit time is approximately 3–5 business days.

Transit times are estimates only.

Actual delivery time may vary depending on:

Destination
Product size and weight
Carrier capacity and routing
Weather
Holidays
Customs or regulatory inspections
Regional service interruptions
Remote or restricted delivery locations
Other circumstances outside our reasonable control
5. Total Estimated Delivery Time

For most standard orders where all required order information is complete, the estimated total time from order placement to delivery is approximately 18–25 business days.

This generally consists of:

15–20 business days for processing and fulfillment
3–5 business days for transportation after shipment

This is a general estimate and not a guaranteed delivery commitment.

Custom projects, made-to-order products, oversized pieces, delayed customer approvals, special delivery requirements, or unexpected logistical conditions may require additional time.

6. Logistics Partners and Carriers

WRYDECO coordinates shipping through third-party logistics providers and selected transportation carriers.

The carrier or delivery method may vary depending on:

Product type
Dimensions and weight
Destination
Required handling
Carrier availability
Delivery conditions

Orders may be transported through recognized carriers or specialist logistics providers, including services such as DHL or other suitable carriers.

WRYDECO does not guarantee that every order will be shipped using a specific carrier.

7. Order Confirmation and Tracking

After placing an order, you will receive an order confirmation using the contact information provided during checkout.

Once your order has shipped, WRYDECO will provide a shipping confirmation and tracking information when tracking is available from the carrier or logistics provider.

Tracking information may require additional time to become active or update after shipment.

Carrier tracking systems are maintained by third parties, and WRYDECO does not control the timing or accuracy of carrier tracking updates.

8. Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping information, including:

Recipient name
Street address
Apartment or suite number, where applicable
City
State or region
Postal code
Telephone number
Delivery instructions reasonably required by the carrier

Please carefully review your shipping information before submitting your order.

WRYDECO is not responsible for delays, failed deliveries, or additional carrier or logistics charges caused by incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or inaccessible delivery information supplied by the customer.

9. Address Changes

Contact WRYDECO as soon as possible if you need to request a shipping address change.

We may be able to update an address only if the order has not entered production, preparation, fulfillment, or shipment.

Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been placed.

Once an order has been transferred to a carrier or logistics provider, any address correction or redirection is subject to carrier approval and may result in additional charges or delays.

10. Delivery Access and Customer Responsibilities

Some WRYDECO products may be large, heavy, oversized, fragile, or require special handling.

Before ordering, customers are responsible for confirming that the product can be safely delivered to and accommodated within the intended location.

Please consider:

Doorways
Hallways
Staircases
Elevators
Loading areas
Property entrances
Room dimensions
Building access requirements

Unless expressly confirmed in writing, standard shipping does not include:

Assembly
Installation
Wall mounting
Furniture placement
Packaging removal
Modification of the customer's property

If delivery cannot be completed because the address is inaccessible, unsafe, incorrect, unattended where attendance is required, or unable to accommodate the product, additional redelivery, storage, return transportation, or related logistics charges may apply.

11. Delivery Appointments

Depending on the product, destination, and carrier, the delivery provider may contact you to arrange a delivery date or delivery window.

Customers are responsible for monitoring communications from WRYDECO and the carrier and for being available when attendance or a signature is required.

Delivery dates and time windows provided by carriers are estimates and cannot be guaranteed.

12. Shipping and Delivery Delays

WRYDECO makes reasonable efforts to process and ship orders within the timeframes stated in this policy.

Delays may occur because of:

Carrier disruptions
Severe weather or natural events
Customs or regulatory inspections
Supply or production delays
Labor disruptions
Transportation shortages
Public emergencies
Incorrect or incomplete customer information
Other circumstances outside our reasonable control

If we learn that we cannot ship an order within the stated or otherwise confirmed processing timeframe, we will notify the customer as required by applicable law.

Where applicable, we will provide an updated shipment estimate and the option to accept the revised timeframe or cancel the affected unshipped order for a refund.

Transit delays occurring after an order has been transferred to the carrier will be reviewed based on the circumstances, carrier information, and applicable law.

13. Inspection Upon Delivery

Please inspect your order as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

If a shipment shows visible damage, where permitted by the carrier:

Examine the external packaging
Note visible damage on the delivery receipt
Photograph the packaging and product
Retain the shipping label
Retain pallets, crates, and protective packaging
Contact WRYDECO promptly

Do not discard the product or packaging until WRYDECO has reviewed the issue and provided further instructions.

14. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from what you ordered, please contact WRYDECO promptly.

Please provide:

Order number
Description of the issue
Photos or video of the product
Photos of the outer and inner packaging
Photo of the shipping label
Relevant carrier or delivery documentation

Claims are reviewed according to our Return & Refund Policy and applicable law.

Natural differences in wood grain, tone, knots, texture, pattern, shape, and organic character are expected characteristics of handcrafted natural wood and are not automatically considered shipping damage or manufacturing defects.

15. Lost or Missing Shipments

If tracking shows no movement for an extended period, or your order has not arrived within a reasonable period after the estimated delivery date, please contact WRYDECO.

We may open an investigation with the carrier or logistics provider.

Carrier investigations may need to be completed before a replacement, refund, or other resolution can be determined.

If tracking shows that your order was delivered but you cannot locate it, please:

Check the delivery area
Ask household members, neighbors, or building staff
Review any delivery photo or carrier notice
Contact the carrier
Contact WRYDECO

Any resolution will depend on the investigation results, carrier records, delivery confirmation, applicable insurance, and applicable law.

16. Refused and Undeliverable Shipments

If a shipment is refused, unclaimed, returned because of an incorrect address, or otherwise determined to be undeliverable for reasons not caused by WRYDECO, the customer may be responsible for resulting:

Return transportation
Redelivery
Storage
Handling
Other carrier or logistics charges

Custom, personalized, and made-to-order products may not be refundable when delivery is refused or cannot be completed for reasons attributable to the customer.

17. Order Cancellations

Cancellation requests must be submitted as soon as possible.

A cancellation may be considered only before production, preparation, or fulfillment has begun.

Cancellation cannot be guaranteed after an order is placed.

Once an order has entered production, preparation, fulfillment, or shipment, it may no longer be eligible for cancellation, modification, return, or refund except where required by applicable law or permitted under our Return & Refund Policy.

18. International Shipping

WRYDECO ships to eligible international destinations available through checkout.

Complimentary standard shipping is included on eligible domestic and international orders, with no minimum purchase requirement.

Customs, Duties, and Import Taxes

International shipments may be subject to customs inspections, import duties, taxes, brokerage fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country or local authorities.

These charges, where applicable, are separate from WRYDECO's $0.00 standard shipping charge.
