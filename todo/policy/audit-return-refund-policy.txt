WRYDECO Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

At WRYDECO, we carefully review claims involving damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled products. Because many of our products are handcrafted, custom, personalized, made-to-order, or produced in natural wood, return eligibility is limited to the circumstances described below.

1. 30-Day Return Eligibility

You may submit a return or product claim within 30 calendar days of delivery only when the item:

Arrives damaged
Arrives defective
Is incorrect because of a WRYDECO fulfillment error

We do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds for:

Change of mind
Personal preference
Incorrect product selection
Incorrect dimensions selected by the customer
Finish or style preference
Other customer-related reasons

All claims are subject to review and approval by WRYDECO.

2. Products That Are Generally Non-Returnable

The following products are not eligible for return for customer-related reasons:

Custom and personalized products
Made-to-order and special-order products
Used, installed, altered, repaired, or customer-damaged products
Sale and clearance products
Gift cards

These restrictions do not prevent WRYDECO from reviewing a valid claim involving approved damage, defect, or fulfillment error.

3. Natural Wood Variations

WRYDECO products may feature natural wood with inherent differences in grain, tone, knots, texture, movement, and organic form.

Minor differences between product photography and the finished handcrafted item are part of the natural character of the material and are not considered defects.

4. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect because of a fulfillment error, please contact WRYDECO within 30 calendar days of delivery.

After reviewing the circumstances and supporting evidence, WRYDECO may provide an appropriate resolution, including:

Repair
Replacement part
Replacement product
Partial refund
Full refund
Authorized return
Another appropriate resolution based on the circumstances

A replacement provided to resolve an approved product issue is treated as a claim resolution and is not a customer-requested exchange.

5. How to Report an Issue

Please contact:

Email: support@wrydeco.com

within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Please provide:

Your full name
Order number
A clear description of the issue
Clear photographs of the product
Photographs of the original packaging
A photograph of the shipping label
Photographs showing any visible damage or defect
Video evidence when the issue is best demonstrated by video

Additional information may be requested when reasonably necessary to review the claim.

6. Preserve the Product and Packaging

Please keep the item and all original packaging while your claim is being reviewed.

Do not:

Discard the product
Return the product
Repair the product
Alter or modify the product
Install the product

until WRYDECO has reviewed the claim and provided written instructions.

Original packaging may also be required for a carrier claim.

7. Approved Claims and Return Instructions

Do not return an item unless WRYDECO specifically instructs you to do so.

A physical return is not required for every approved claim.

If WRYDECO determines that a return is necessary, we will provide the applicable return or collection instructions.

For an approved claim involving verified damage, defect, or an incorrect item caused by a fulfillment error, WRYDECO will cover the approved return shipping costs.

WRYDECO does not provide or cover return shipping for change-of-mind, preference-based, or other customer-related returns because those returns are not accepted.

8. Exchanges

WRYDECO does not accept exchanges based on:

Change of mind
Personal preference
Incorrect customer selection
Dimensions, finish, or style selected by the customer

For an approved damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled item, WRYDECO may provide a replacement or corrected product as part of the claim resolution.

9. Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

When a physical return is required, WRYDECO will process the approved refund within 2 business days after the returned item has been received and inspected.

Depending on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider, it may take up to 3 additional business days for the refund to appear in your account.

10. Order Cancellations

Cancellation or modification requests may be considered only before production, preparation, or fulfillment has begun.

Once production has started, custom and made-to-order products cannot be canceled, changed, returned, or refunded for customer-related reasons.

Please contact WRYDECO as soon as possible with your order number if you need to request a cancellation or modification.

Cancellation cannot be guaranteed after an order has been placed.

11. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be waived.

Where applicable law provides rights that differ from this policy, those mandatory legal rights will apply.
